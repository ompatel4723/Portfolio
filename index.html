My Journey: Resilience, Discipline, & Code

I built my academic foundation at B.S. Patel Primary School in India in the CBSE STEM track from Jr. KG through mid-12th grade. Relocated to the U.S. in May 2026 as a Permanent Resident to finish senior year at J.P. Stevens High School. This imposed a major challenge in front of me.

Taking required classes with underclassmen gave me two completely opposite experiences. The freshmen in my Bio class were amazing—they were shocked to have a senior sitting with them, but they were super kind, welcoming, and made coming to a new school feel easy. US History with sophomores, on the other hand, was a massive test of my patience. With teammates showing zero accountability and leaving everything until the final night, I ended up carrying most of workload to save my grade. Juggling my college applications while doing everyone else's work was exhausting, but it proved that when group coordination breaks down, I can step up and get the job done.

My passion for Computer Science started with a deep childhood love for video games. The moment I realized that every game, website, and app is built line-by-line through code written by real engineers, I was completely hooked—thinking, "HOW COOL!" That single realization sparked my eagerness to dive into coding and master software engineering.
